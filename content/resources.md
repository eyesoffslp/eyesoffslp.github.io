**Here are some external resources that you might find useful:**

* [DeFlock](https://deflock.org/) - A hub for education, mapping, and more resources related to Flock and ALPR.
* [DeFlock MPLS](https://www.deflockmpls.com/) - A Minneapolis-specific DeFlock effort.
* [SLP ALPR Policy](https://www.stlouisparkmn.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/28047/638681458304130000) - The official policy document for ALPR use in St. Louis Park.
* [SLP Flock Transparency Portal](https://transparency.flocksafety.com/saint-louis-park-mn-pd) - A Flock data transparency portal published for St. Louis Park.
* [Rural Privacy ALPR Toolkit](https://ruralprivacy.org/alpr-toolkit) -  Templates and tutorials to help push back against ALPR.
* [Flock Privacy Request Portal](https://privacyportal.onetrust.com/webform/abd283d9-9d03-4d74-aa5b-3529f7216767/9669345b-843e-48d3-aa6b-5edf2d1e9c9b) - A portal to submit privacy requests to Flock.
