Contact Your City Council

The mayor and two at large council members represent all St. Louis Park community members. The four ward representatives, while working on behalf of all of St. Louis Park, specifically represent those who live and work in their respective wards.

Please contact the Mayor, at large council members, and your ward’s representative to share your concerns about the risks that Flock brings to our community, and why St. Louis Park residents deserve for the city to end their use.

Find your Ward

To find your ward, view the ward map (opens PDF) or use the ward and precinct finder tool (under “Precinct & Districts” listed as ST LOUIS PARK W-1, W-2, W-3, or W-4).

Use the Progress in the Park Email Campaign Template

Previously, Progress in the Park opened an email campaign to push for ALPR policy changes ahead of an April 2026 city council study session.

Visit this site to read about the demands specific to that campaign, which stop short of asking for the city to end or reduce its usage of Flock or any ALPR system. If those points resonate with you, you can use that page to send a templated email directly from the embedded form.
