Tell Your City: Flock Out!

We are calling on the St. Louis Park City Council to terminate the Flock contract and conduct an ethical review of ALPR use in the city.

Our demands are:

  1. Terminate the current Flock Safety contract.
  2. Require independent technical and ethical reviews of alternative vendors by non-police oversight bodies, such as the Community Technology Advisory Commission or the Police Advisory Commission, prior to any selection.
  3. Establish a mandatory public engagement and community input process at the inception of any future surveillance proposals, rather than relying solely on late-stage public comment sessions.
  4. Designate a qualified city data steward to oversee, audit, and evaluate the privacy and security impacts of all existing and proposed municipal data collection initiatives.
